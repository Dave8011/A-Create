🔐 Amazon SP-API EU / Global Configuration

Configure your Selling Partner API credentials for publishing A+ content to Amazon.

🛡️
Zero-Exposure Security Guarantee: All credentials (Client ID, Secret, Refresh Token, Seller ID) are managed strictly server-side in your protected .env file (file mode 0600). Secrets are never returned over API endpoints or exposed in browser consoles.
💡
Credentials Persistence: Your credentials are saved both in your browser's local storage and in .env. You will not lose your settings when refreshing the page.
ℹ️ Fixing 403 Forbidden ("Access to requested resource is denied")
If upload destinations or catalog sync fail with 403:
  1. In Amazon Seller Central, go to Partner Network → Develop Apps.
  2. Click Edit App on your registered app, and verify that the Product Listing role is checked.
  3. Critical Step: After adding the role, click Authorize next to your app in Develop Apps to generate an updated Refresh Token (refresh tokens only carry roles granted at authorization time).
  4. Paste the new Refresh Token below, click Save Settings, then run Diagnose Permissions to verify!
